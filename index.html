Point of sale, stock and simple accounting for Nigerian businesses

NaijaPOS gives your shop a barcode-ready point of sale, multi-branch inventory, purchases, customer records, expenses and simple accounting. Subscription in Naira, no hardware lock-in.

What you get on day one

  • Sell at the counter with a barcode scanner
  • Track stock across shops and warehouses
  • Purchases, suppliers and purchase returns
  • Customers, credit sales and WhatsApp follow-up
  • Expenses with categories
  • Simple accounting: chart of accounts, P&L, balance sheet
  • Staff users with scoped permissions
  • Daily sales and stock reports for the owner

Bring your own hardware

Use the computer, hand scanner and Moniepoint/card terminal you already own. Card payments settle on your terminal; NaijaPOS records the sale as cash or bank transfer.

Setup in three steps

  1. Pick a subdomain and plan
  2. Pay the first month by card or bank transfer
  3. Your workspace is created automatically — import products and sell
